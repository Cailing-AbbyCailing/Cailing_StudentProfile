INTRODUCTION

Hello! My name is Abegail P. Cailing, and I am an only child who recently turned 20 years old. I live in Binitinan, Balingasag, Misamis Oriental, and I am currently studying at Xavier University as a BSIT student.

Interests

I have many hobbies and interests, but most of them somehow connect to art and its meaning. Because of this, I became very interested in designing and structuring layouts, especially when I can challenge myself to create something new. I enjoy the process of turning my ideas into something that I can actually see and use. What I enjoy most is the satisfaction of finishing something I created and seeing it function properly. Whether it is a web layout, a design, or one of my projects, I like being able to combine creativity and technology to create something that represents my ideas and skills. Unfortunately I was not given a gift of creativity but that wont stop me from trying.

Educational Background

I began my education at Binitinan Elementary School, where I once dreamed of becoming a professional teacher. Growing up, I was inspired by my parents, who were both teachers. Watching them work made me appreciate the dedication and responsibility that come along with the profession. I also saw how challenging their work could be, especially during difficult situations such as disasters, when they were still required to report to work and check on their responsibilities. Seeing how hard it could be, it made me reconsider my dream and choose a different career path. Then, I continued my education at St. Rita’s College of Balingasag, where I completed both my junior and senior high school years. I passed the requirements to become a STEM student, which led me to take a risk and choose a path that separated me from some of my friends. Although it was a difficult decision, my experiences in STEM helped me develop an interest in technology and encouraged me to explore a different path. Because of my curiosity and growing interest in technology, I am now pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology at Xavier University.

Goals or Aspirations

My goal is to become a web developer, where I can design and develop web pages that reflect my own creativity and style. I aspire to combine my technical skills with my personal approach to design to create websites that are both functional and visually appealing. In the future, I want to bring together my skills, ideas, and projects into work that I can be proud of while also creating websites that meet the needs and expectations of clients and users.
